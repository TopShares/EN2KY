
技巧 5：转折之后是重点
转折词是观点变化、强调重点、引出结论的信号。



类型
强转折 but, however, yet, nevertheless, nonetheless
让步转折 although, though, even if, despite, in spite of, while
对比转折 on the contrary, by contrast, conversely, rather than, unlike
隐性转折  in fact, actually, as a matter of fact, the truth is (看似陈述事实，实为推翻前文)
